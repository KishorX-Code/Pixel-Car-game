## 3D-Endless-Road-Car-Game  
A copy of Car game.
----------
How it works:  
GamePlay is Simple as the car Game, In this game Drive your car at endless Highway which has ai traffic car,The main objective of this game is to survive for a long time without any collisions with ai car and other obstacle.

_______________________________
## Features
. Endless highway  
. AI- car  
. Car left right  
. Car move forward and reverse  
. Car collision and engine sound  
. Game over and restart button  
______________________________________
## Controls
. W / Up Arrow – Accelerate   
. S / Down Arrow – Brake / Reverse   
. A / Left Arrow – Left   
. D / Right Arrow – Right
